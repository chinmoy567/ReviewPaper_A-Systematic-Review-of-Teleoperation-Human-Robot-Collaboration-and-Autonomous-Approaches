## Journal of Field Robotics: author guidelines (Wiley page, "updated March 2026")
URL: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/15564967/homepage/forauthors.html
- **Article types:** research paper, **review paper**, research note, field report, invited paper. The scope lists search and rescue and human-robot interaction, and explicitly encourages "comparative or meta-studies" and validation of autonomous systems in representative environments. This is a direct fit for the review's validation-gap theme.
- **Free Format submission:** any consistent reference style is accepted at first submission. The final style is APA (6th ed.) author-date, alphabetical, with DOIs where available.
- **Abstract** of 250 words or fewer; **seven keywords**; US spelling; no footnotes; tables and figures embedded in the main text.
- **Title page:** co-author details (affiliation and email); data availability statement; funding statement; conflict-of-interest disclosure.
- **ORCID** is required for the submitting author.
- **Data sharing:** the journal expects data to be archived in a public repository, with a data availability statement linking to it; scripts should also be archived where possible.
- **Funding** goes in the Acknowledgments. Use the Crossref Funder Registry names.
- **Ethics:** COPE member; text is checked with iThenticate CrossCheck.
- Open access is optional, with an APC.
