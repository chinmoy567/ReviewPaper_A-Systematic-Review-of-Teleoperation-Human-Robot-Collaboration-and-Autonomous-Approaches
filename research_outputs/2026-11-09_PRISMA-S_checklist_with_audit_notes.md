**How to read this file.**
- Item text is **paraphrased** from the PRISMA-S checklist; for the authoritative wording see the article above.
- "Location" refers to the manuscript (Section II-B = Information Sources and Search Strategy; II-C = Deduplication) and to the supplementary search protocol (`supplementary/search_protocol.md`, abbreviated SP).
- The search reported here is the search **actually run** for this review: one author searching seven publisher platforms, Google Scholar, and two large language models (ChatGPT, Gemini). Rows marked "Supplementary search (if run)" describe what changes if the authors run the database search specified in SP Sec. 2; until then they stay empty.
- The column "Audit note (delete before submission)" gives the 2026-11-09 audit status: R = reported; P = partially reported; M = missing; N/A = not applicable.
