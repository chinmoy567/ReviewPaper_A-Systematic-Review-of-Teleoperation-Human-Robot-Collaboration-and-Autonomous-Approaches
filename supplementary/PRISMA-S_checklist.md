- The search reported here is the search **actually run** for this review: one author searching seven publisher platforms, Google Scholar, and two large language models (ChatGPT, Gemini). Rows marked "Supplementary search (if run)" describe what changes if the authors run the database search specified in SP Sec. 2; until then they stay empty.
